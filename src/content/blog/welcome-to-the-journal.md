Assalamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh.

Social media is useful for sharing a recitation or a short reminder, but some thoughts deserve more time and space. This journal is where I hope to share longer reflections connected to the Qur’an, lessons from hadith, and the questions Muslims encounter in everyday life.

My intention is not to present myself as a scholar. Where a subject requires knowledge beyond my own, I will aim to rely on and clearly reference qualified scholarship. This space is for sincere reflection, continued learning, and encouraging one another to return to the words of Allah.

I also want listeners to be part of the conversation. If an ayah has stayed with you, a recitation has helped you through a particular moment, or there is a subject you would value seeing explored, you can share it through the Listening Circle form.

May this become a beneficial space, and may Allah place sincerity and goodness in it.
